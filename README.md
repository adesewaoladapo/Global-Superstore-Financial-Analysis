# Global Superstore Financial & Profitability Analysis

## Executive Summary
An executive financial assessment analyzing $2.17M in total revenue across 1,000 global retail orders to uncover profitability leaks, discount erosion thresholds, and shipping cost inefficiencies.

## Assessment Findings
- **Profit Leaks:** The **Supplies** sub-category generates the highest net loss across the business (**-$89.85** net loss).
- **Discount Threshold Trap:** Order profitability flips from positive earnings into net financial losses at discounts equal to or exceeding **30% (0.30)**.
- **Shipping Burden:** **Asia Pacific - Standard Class** shipping incurs the highest single regional logistics cost (**$40,323.73** across 134 orders).
- **KPI Summary:** Total Revenue: **$2.17M** | Net Profit: **$286.4K** | Profit Margin: **13.21%**.
